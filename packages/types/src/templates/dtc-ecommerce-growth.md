# DTC eCommerce Marketing Strategy — Growth

**Stage**: $1K–$10K/month marketing budget | $50K–$500K/month revenue | 500–5K total customers | Repeat purchase rate validated (10%+)

## Goals
- Scale revenue from $50K to $250K+/month through owned channels (email/SMS) and diversified paid media
- Achieve LTV:CAC ratio ≥3:1 with payback period <6 months (shorter than B2B)
- Build a direct customer relationship through email and SMS (email + SMS = 25–45% of total revenue at maturity)
- Optimize unit economics: reduce customer acquisition cost while increasing average order value and repeat purchase rate

## Audience
- **Primary**: Fashion, lifestyle, and wellness-focused consumers aged 25–45 who shop online 2+ times/month; primarily female (60–70% typical for DTC)
- **Secondary**: Gift buyers, trend-conscious early adopters (aged 18–55) discovering new brands via social media
- **Pain points**: Overwhelmed by choice (decision fatigue); skeptical of new brands (trust barriers); seeking authentic, values-aligned shopping experiences; desire for personalization and convenience
- **Watering holes**: Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube (influencers and unboxing videos), email (owned channel), SMS (owned channel), Facebook/Meta (retargeting), Google Shopping, Reddit (niche communities)

## Channels

### Channel Mix at This Stage (Paid 60–70%, Owned 25–35%, Organic 5–10%)

1. **Email + SMS** (type: owned) — The profit center of DTC. Average email+SMS = 25–45% of total revenue at scale. Primary metric: Revenue per email, SMS conversion rate, LTV by cohort, repeat purchase rate. Budget: 5–10% (operational, not acquisition—ROI is 40:1+).
   - Focus: Welcome series, post-purchase flows (review requests, cross-sell, upsell), abandoned cart, seasonal/promotional campaigns, SMS flash sales
   - Measure: RPM (revenue per thousand emails), SMS conversion rate, email list growth rate, churn rate

2. **Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram)** (type: paid) — Dominant DTC channel. Scale through product catalog ads, lookalike audiences, sequential messaging, and dynamic retargeting. Primary metric: ROAS (return on ad spend), CAC, LTV:CAC ratio. Budget: 30–40%.
   - Focus: New customer acquisition (broad interests, lookalikes), retargeting warm audiences (website visitors, cart abandoners, video viewers), customer acquisition by product line
   - Measure: ROAS by campaign and audience; CAC by cohort; LTV:CAC; payback period

3. **Google Shopping + Search Ads** (type: paid) — High-intent, bottom-funnel channel. Primary metric: ROAS, CAC, conversion rate. Budget: 15–20%.
   - Focus: Brand keywords (protect against competitors), high-AOV product keywords, seasonal/event-driven searches, remarketing
   - Measure: ROAS by product category; CAC by keyword intent; average order value by campaign

4. **Influencer Partnerships** (type: earned/paid) — Micro-influencers (10K–100K followers) and affiliate relationships. Primary metric: Customer CAC, LTV by influencer, referral conversion rate. Budget: 10–15%.
   - Focus: Micro-influencers (better ROI than macro); product seeding; affiliate partnerships with commissions; UGC repurposing
   - Measure: CAC by influencer; lifetime value of influencer-acquired customers; attribution via unique codes/links

5. **Organic Social & UGC** (type: owned) — Instagram Reels, TikTok, Pinterest for brand awareness and content discovery. Primary metric: Engagement rate, traffic to site, brand recall. Budget: 5–10% (content creation, not paid).
   - Focus: UGC reposts, user-generated content creation, Reels testing, Pinterest SEO (product pins), TikTok original content
   - Measure: Engagement rate, save rate, click-through rate to site, traffic from organic social

6. **Retargeting (Programmatic Display + Video)** (type: paid) — Re-engage warm audiences across the web. Primary metric: Conversion rate, CAC, ROAS. Budget: 5–10%.
   - Focus: Website visitor lists, video viewers, content consumers, cart abandoners
   - Measure: CAC vs. paid acquisition channels; conversion rate by audience warm-ness

### DTC Unit Economics: The Metrics That Matter

| Metric | Target | Benchmark | Notes |
|--------|--------|-----------|-------|
| **Average Order Value (AOV)** | $144–$200 | DTC avg: $144–$153 | Higher AOV = lower CAC needed for 3:1 LTV:CAC |
| **Repeat Purchase Rate (12-month)** | 25–35% | DTC avg: 25–30% | Core growth lever; direct impact on LTV |
| **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)** | $600–$1,200 | Depends on AOV + repeat rate | (AOV × repeat purchase rate × gross margin %) / (1 + discount rate) |
| **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** | $20–$60 | Varies by channel | Paid avg: $40; organic avg: $5 |
| **LTV:CAC Ratio** | ≥3:1 | DTC standard: 3–5:1 | Minimum viability; 5:1 indicates profitable scaling |
| **Payback Period** | <6 months | DTC avg: 3–6 months | Time to recover CAC from gross margin |
| **Gross Margin %** | 60–75% | DTC avg: 60–70% | Cost of goods + fulfillment |
| **Email + SMS % of Revenue** | 25–45% | DTC avg: 30% at maturity | Owned channel ROI: 40:1+ |
| **Return Rate** | <10% | DTC avg: 5–10% | Impacts true LTV and margins |
| **Cart Abandonment Rate** | <60% | DTC avg: 60–80% | Email recovery converts 10–15% of abandonments |

### Marketing Efficiency: The DTC Reality (2024–2025)

- **ROAS declining ~9% YoY across DTC** (increased competition for ad inventory)
- **Customer acquisition cost rising ~15–20% YoY** (ad inflation)
- **Fixed marketing costs rising ~32% YoY** (design, tools, content creation)
- **Implication**: Volume + efficiency (owned channels) become increasingly important; paid channel efficiency must improve or shift to owned

## Content

### Pillars
- **Product education & use-case demos**: How-to videos, styling guides, product comparisons, benefits education
- **Founder/brand story**: Values-aligned narrative, sustainability, craftsmanship, behind-the-scenes content (builds trust for new customers)
- **Customer testimonials & UGC**: Reviews, testimonials, unboxing videos, styling photos from customers (social proof for conversion)
- **Seasonal & trend-driven content**: Holiday campaigns, seasonal collections, trending aesthetics, limited-edition drops

### Cadence
- **Email**: 3–5 emails/week (mix of promotional, educational, and value-driven)
- **SMS**: 2–4 SMS/month (flash sales, limited-time offers, VIP exclusives)
- **Instagram Reels**: 3–5 Reels/week
- **TikTok**: 2–4 videos/week (if audience present)
- **Pinterest**: 5–10 pins/week (evergreen product pins, seasonal collections)
- **YouTube**: 1–2 videos/month (longer-form tutorials, hauls, brand story)

### Formats
- **Email**: Welcome series (5–7 emails over 2 weeks), abandoned cart (2–3 emails), post-purchase (review request, cross-sell, loyalty), seasonal campaigns
- **SMS**: Flash sales (limited-time code), VIP early access, reorder reminders, birthday offers
- **Reels/TikTok**: 15–60 sec videos; formats: product demos, styling tips, trending sounds + product, customer testimonials, trending challenges
- **UGC/testimonials**: 30–60 sec customer videos in their own words (repurposed in ads, email, socials)
- **Pinterest**: Product pins (high-quality photography), infographics, style guides
- **YouTube**: 5–15 min tutorials, hauls, brand story mini-docs, product comparisons

## Schedule

### Months 1–3: Foundation & Email/SMS Optimization
- **Theme**: "Maximize owned channel revenue (email + SMS); baseline paid channel efficiency; understand unit economics."
- **Actions**:
  - Audit existing email list; segment by purchase recency, AOV, product preference; implement segment-specific campaigns
  - Build comprehensive email funnel: welcome series (5–7 emails), browse abandonment, cart abandonment (2–3 emails), post-purchase (review request + cross-sell), re-engagement campaigns
  - Implement SMS opt-in; build SMS welcome series (3–4 messages over 7 days); plan SMS flash sales and VIP offers
  - Calculate unit economics for each channel: AOV, repeat purchase rate by cohort, LTV, CAC, ROAS, payback period
  - Implement UTM tagging across all campaigns; ensure email + SMS tracked separately
  - Review Meta Ads account: audit campaigns, audiences, creative performance; identify underperforming audiences; kill low-ROAS campaigns
  - Launch 3 Google Shopping campaigns segmented by product category (high-AOV, mid-range, bundles); budget $200–300/month
  - Set up UGC collection: branded hashtag, incentive for reviews/testimonials, email request to customers
  - Implement basic product recommendation engine in email (bestsellers, "most bought with," post-purchase recommendations)
- **Success criteria**:
  - Email ROI: 40:1 or higher (typical DTC; calculate as email revenue / email send costs)
  - SMS conversion rate: 3–5% (typical for SMS)
  - Email list growth: 5–10%/month (new customers + organic signups)
  - SMS list: 500+ subscribers
  - Meta Ads ROAS: 2.5:1 or higher (break-even + modest profit)
  - Google Shopping ROAS: 2:1 or higher
  - Unit economics documented: CAC, LTV, payback period, and LTV:CAC ratio calculated by channel
  - UGC library: 20+ customer videos/testimonials collected
- **Decision point**:
  - Which product categories have highest AOV and repeat purchase rate? (Prioritize in paid ads)
  - Which email segment is most engaged? (Increase email frequency to top segment)
  - Meta Ads or Google Shopping underperforming? (Pivot budget to other channel or fix targeting/creative)
  - Is LTV:CAC ratio <3:1? (If yes, pause paid acquisition; focus on owned channels + unit economics)

### Months 4–6: Paid Channel Scaling & Influencer Partnerships
- **Theme**: "Scale profitable paid channels; diversify with influencer partnerships; increase repeat purchase rate."
- **Actions**:
  - Scale Meta Ads: increase budget by 25–50% to best-performing audiences (lookalikes from repeat customers, high-AOV buyers); test new creative (video, carousel, dynamic ads)
  - Launch retargeting campaign: website visitors, video viewers, add-to-cart non-converters; budget $100–200/month
  - Implement sequential messaging on Meta: awareness → consideration → conversion (3–5 ad sequence over 14 days)
  - Increase Google Shopping budget by 25–50% to $300–500/month; test bid strategies (target ROAS vs. maximize conversions)
  - Launch influencer outreach: identify 10–15 micro-influencers (10K–100K followers) in your niche; negotiate product seeding or affiliate partnerships
  - Deploy influencer-generated content in Meta Ads: repurpose influencer videos as ad creative (24–48% higher ROAS typical)
  - Implement post-purchase email expansion: review request email (trigger after delivery), "here's what others bought next" (cross-sell), loyalty program enrollment
  - Test SMS flash sales: 2x/month limited-time offers (48–72 hour window) with exclusive SMS code
  - Increase email frequency: test 4–5 emails/week (if engagement holds, continue; if unsubscribe rate rises, reduce)
  - Launch referral program: customer → friend incentive (e.g., $15 credit for referring customer who spends $50+)
- **Success criteria**:
  - Meta Ads ROAS: 3:1 or higher (profitable scaling threshold)
  - Google Shopping ROAS: 2.5:1 or higher
  - Influencer CAC: <$30 per customer (typically lower than paid ads)
  - Repeat purchase rate: 30%+ (up from baseline; measure by cohort)
  - Email revenue: 30%+ of total revenue (owned channel focus paying off)
  - SMS revenue: 5–10% of total revenue
  - LTV:CAC ratio: 3.5:1 or better across all channels
  - Referral pipeline: 5–10% of new customers (track with unique codes)
  - UGC library: 50+ customer testimonials; repurpose 2–3 per week in ads and email
- **Decision point**:
  - Which influencer partnerships are driving lowest CAC? (Scale budget with those creators)
  - Is repeat purchase rate improving through email/SMS? (If yes, shift more budget to owned channels; if no, optimize email content/offers)
  - Meta Ads ROAS declining? (Test new audiences, creative refresh, or bid strategy adjustment)
  - Is payback period still <6 months? (If payback extending, reduce paid spend; focus on owned channels)

### Months 7–9: Product-Level Optimization & SMS Expansion
- **Theme**: "Optimize by product line; expand SMS; test new paid channels; improve average order value."
- **Actions**:
  - Product-level attribution: calculate ROAS, CAC, LTV, repeat purchase rate by product line
  - Increase marketing spend on high-LTV products (high AOV + high repeat rate); reduce spend on low-repeat products
  - Launch SMS sequence expansion: SMS birthday offers, SMS reorder reminders (based on purchase history), SMS exclusive VIP sales
  - Implement dynamic pricing or bundling experiments: test "frequently bought together" offers, tiered discounts, mystery bundle deals
  - Test Pinterest Ads: product catalog ads targeting high-intent pinners; budget $100–200/month; measure CAC and ROAS
  - Expand influencer program: move top-performing affiliates to ongoing partnerships (vs. one-time seeding); introduce influencer-exclusive discount codes
  - Launch customer loyalty program: points per purchase, exclusive perks, tiered rewards (bronze/silver/gold); integrate with email + SMS
  - Create product-specific email campaigns: high-repeat customers get "here's your favorite" recommendations; new customers get education series
  - Optimize checkout and post-purchase experience: reduce cart abandonment rate through exit-intent offers, guest checkout, multiple payment methods
- **Success criteria**:
  - ROAS by product line calculated; top 3 products driving 50%+ of revenue identified
  - SMS revenue: 10–15% of total revenue (increasing)
  - Email + SMS combined: 35%+ of total revenue
  - Repeat purchase rate: 32–35%+ (continuing to improve)
  - LTV:CAC ratio: 3.5–4:1 across all channels
  - Pinterest Ads ROAS: 2:1 or higher (if tested)
  - Influencer-sourced revenue: 10–15% of new customer revenue
  - Loyalty program: 1K+ members enrolled; repeat purchase rate among members: 40%+ (vs. 30% baseline)
  - Average order value: 10–15% increase through bundling/upsell offers
- **Decision point**:
  - Which product line should be the hero in paid campaigns? (Based on LTV + repeat rate)
  - Should we expand SMS frequency based on engagement and revenue contribution?
  - Is Pinterest Ads worth scaling, or should we reallocate budget?
  - Are we hitting email fatigue (declining open rates, rising unsubscribe rate)? (If yes, reduce frequency or improve content relevance)

### Months 10–12: Scaling & Retention Focus
- **Theme**: "Optimize for profitable scaling; shift focus to retention and repeat purchase; plan Year 2."
- **Actions**:
  - Consolidate winning channels: scale Meta Ads and Google Shopping budgets to $1K–$2K+/month (if payback <6 months and LTV:CAC >3:1)
  - Launch Facebook Conversions API and iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) compliance: improve data accuracy on Meta Ads
  - Test new cohort strategy: segment customers by acquisition month and cohort; compare LTV, repeat purchase, churn by cohort and source
  - Implement predictive analytics: identify at-risk customers (declining engagement); send targeted retention emails or win-back offers
  - Expand SMS: test SMS pre-purchase (product launch teasers, waitlist), post-purchase (shipping notifications, review requests), and retention (exclusive SMS-only codes)
  - Create annual customer report or "Year in Review" campaign: send personalized recap of purchases, suggest products based on history
  - Build affiliate program: formalize relationships with top influencers and content creators; offer 10–20% commission per sale
  - Plan Year 2: forecast revenue by channel, set targets for CAC reduction and LTV increase, plan new channel tests (TikTok Shop, Amazon affiliate, partnerships)
  - Document playbooks: email cadence by segment, SMS calendar, paid ad strategy, influencer onboarding process
  - Hire or train marketing operations person: ensure consistency, data quality, and process documentation
- **Success criteria**:
  - Blended ROAS across all paid channels: 3:1 or higher
  - Email + SMS revenue: 40–45% of total revenue
  - Repeat purchase rate: 32–40%+
  - LTV:CAC ratio: 4:1 or better
  - Payback period: <5 months
  - Customer retention rate (12-month cohort): 25–35% (repeat purchase rate measure)
  - CAC by channel: Meta $20–40, Google Shopping $15–30, influencer $15–25
  - Influencer revenue: 15–25% of new customer revenue
  - Loyalty program: 2K–3K active members; repeat rate 45%+
  - Year 2 forecast: 40–100% revenue growth (based on channel scaling + repeat purchase improvements)
- **Decision point**:
  - Which channels are most efficient and have best LTV:CAC ratios? (Allocate 70% of Year 2 budget here)
  - Should we build TikTok Shop presence or focus on Meta + Google?
  - Is paid channel saturation approaching? (If ROAS declining >20%, shift focus to owned channels + retention)
  - Do we need a full-time marketer or hiring an agency for Year 2?
  - **PMF Velocity Check**: If weekly order growth drops below 5% for 3+ consecutive weeks OR LTV:CAC falls below 2:1, pause paid scaling and investigate churn/positioning before resuming. See `framework-selection-matrix.md` §PMF Velocity Override.

## DTC Growth Principles

1. **Owned channels (email + SMS) are your moat**: Paid channels get expensive; owned channels (email + SMS) are defensible competitive advantages. Aim for 40%+ of revenue from owned channels by Month 12.

2. **Repeat customers are your profit engine**: A 30% repeat purchase rate delivers 60–70% of LTV. Invest heavily in email + SMS to increase repeat rate and AOV among existing customers.

3. **Unit economics drive decisions**: Track LTV:CAC ratio, payback period, ROAS by channel, and repeat purchase rate obsessively. When efficiency declines, shift budget toward owned channels and retention.

4. **Influencers >> Brands in DTC**: Micro-influencers (10K–100K followers) typically deliver 2–3x lower CAC than paid ads. Prioritize influencer partnerships and UGC repurposing.

5. **Creative refresh is non-negotiable**: Ad performance degrades 10–30%/month without fresh creative. Plan creative testing and rotation every 2–4 weeks.

6. **CAC rising, ROAS declining**: Expect ~9–15% YoY ROAS decline and ~15–20% CAC increase. Budget accordingly. Build unit economics to sustain profitability at higher CAC.

## Year 2 Planning (After Months 10–12)

Once you've achieved:
- Email + SMS = 40%+ of revenue
- LTV:CAC ratio ≥4:1
- Repeat purchase rate 30%+
- Payback period <5 months

Consider scaling:
- **TikTok Shop or Instagram Shop**: Reduce friction in conversion funnel
- **Amazon affiliate or marketplace presence**: New distribution channel
- **Strategic partnerships**: Co-marketing with complementary brands
- **Affiliate network expansion**: Formalize and incentivize top performers
- **Video production scaling**: UGC + influencer video library
- **International expansion**: Test 1–2 new geographies
- **Full-time marketer or agency**: As marketing operations and complexity increase
