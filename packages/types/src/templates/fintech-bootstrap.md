# Fintech Marketing Strategy — Bootstrap

## Goals
- Build trust and credibility in a highly regulated, trust-sensitive market
- Acquire first 100 users through education-led content and strategic partnerships
- Establish a repeatable inbound pipeline with <$1K/mo spend

## Audience
- **Primary**: Finance-forward SMB owners and solo operators (accountants, bookkeepers, freelance CFOs) looking for modern tooling
- **Secondary**: Tech-savvy consumers frustrated with legacy banking/payments UX
- **Pain points**: Compliance anxiety, fragmented financial data, slow onboarding at incumbents, lack of transparency in fees
- **Watering holes**: LinkedIn (CFO and accounting communities), r/accounting and r/smallbusiness on Reddit, QuickBooks community forums

## Channels
1. **Content/SEO** (type: owned) — Educational long-form content targeting "how to" and compliance-related searches. Primary metric: Organic search traffic to calculator and compliance guides. Budget: 40%.
2. **Strategic Partnerships** (type: earned) — Co-marketing with adjacent SaaS (accounting, payroll, invoicing tools). Primary metric: Partner-driven traffic and qualified referrals. Budget: 25%.
3. **LinkedIn + Twitter/X** (type: owned) — Thought leadership from founders on fintech trends, regulatory changes, and product updates. Primary metric: Engagement rate and follower growth. Budget: 35%.

## Compliance Gate
All marketing content requires review before publication:
- **Risk tier 1 (low)**: Brand awareness, general education → Self-review + checklist (same-day)
- **Risk tier 2 (medium)**: Product features, pricing comparisons, ROI claims → Legal review (5 business days)
- **Risk tier 3 (high)**: Regulatory explainers, compliance guides, financial claims → Legal + compliance review (5–8 business days)

⚠️ FTC requires substantiation for all express and implied claims. Recent enforcement: $33M Biz2Credit settlement (2024) for unsubstantiated claims. All ROI calculators and comparison content require documented substantiation before publishing.

## Content
### Pillars
- Regulatory explainers and compliance guides (demystify complex topics)
- ROI calculators and comparison content (vs. incumbent banks/tools)
- Founder transparency posts (build-in-public for trust)

### Cadence
- Content/SEO: 2 articles/month
- LinkedIn: 2 posts/week
- Twitter/X: 3 posts/week

### Formats
- Long-form SEO articles (1,500–2,500 words)
- Calculator landing pages (interactive tools with lead capture)
- Founder short-form posts (LinkedIn, Twitter)
- Case studies (3,000+ words with metrics)

## Schedule

### Months 1–2: Foundation & Trust Building
- **Actions**: Publish 4 SEO articles targeting compliance and "how to" queries. Create ROI calculator landing page. Start LinkedIn founder posting 2x/week.
- **Success criteria**: 200+ organic visitors/month to SEO articles; 50+ calculator leads captured
- **Decision point**: If SEO articles <50 visitors/month after 6 weeks, pivot to guest posting on established fintech publications

### Months 3–4: Partnerships & Case Studies
- **Actions**: Launch co-marketing campaign with 1–2 adjacent SaaS partners (webinar, shared blog post). Build email nurture sequence. Publish first customer case study.
- **Success criteria**: 100+ webinar attendees; 30+ email subscribers; case study on website
- **Decision point**: If partner webinar <50 attendees, focus exclusive energy on organic SEO content instead

### Months 5–6: Scaling & Testing
- **Actions**: Apply to 2–3 fintech directories. Publish 2 additional SEO articles. Test 1 LinkedIn Live session on regulatory topic.
- **Success criteria**: 5+ directory listings live; 100+ organic visits from directories; LinkedIn Live 50+ attendees
- **Decision point**: If directory traffic is minimal, reallocate effort to content marketing and partnerships
- **PMF Velocity Override**: If weekly signup growth >15% AND MRR >$500, graduate to Growth tier regardless of calendar phase. See `framework-selection-matrix.md` §PMF Velocity Override.
