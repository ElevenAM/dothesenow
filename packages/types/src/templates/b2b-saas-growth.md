# B2B SaaS Marketing Strategy — Growth

**Stage**: $1K–$10K/month marketing budget | $100K–$1M ARR | 20–100 existing customers | PMF validated

## Goals
- Scale revenue from $100K to $500K+ ARR through diversified, data-driven channels
- Achieve LTV:CAC ratio ≥3:1 with payback period <12 months
- Build a predictable, multi-channel acquisition engine with clear attribution
- Establish product and company as category leaders in target buyer mind

## Audience
- **Primary**: Technical decision-makers and procurement (CTOs, VP Eng, Directors of Infrastructure) at mid-market companies (50–500 employees)
- **Secondary**: Non-technical founders and product leaders evaluating strategic tools; procurement/finance evaluating business cases
- **Pain points**: Tool sprawl and integration complexity; lack of visibility into team/infrastructure performance; high switching costs from incumbents; ROI justification pressure from finance
- **Watering holes**: LinkedIn (group memberships, job targeting), Hacker News and Product Hunt (for credibility and word-of-mouth), industry-specific Slack communities and Discord servers, dev-focused YouTube channels, GitHub, Twitter/X (#buildinpublic and specific hashtags)

## Channels

### Channel Mix at This Stage (70% proven, 20% optimization, 10% experimentation)

1. **Content/SEO** (type: owned) — Target informational, comparison, and "how to" keywords at scale. Primary metric: Organic search volume, traffic to comparison/demo pages, organic-sourced SQL. Budget: 25–30%.
   - Focus: High-intent keywords (e.g., "vs. [competitor]"), problem-aware searches (e.g., "how to reduce infrastructure costs"), bottom-funnel decision content
   - Measure: CAC by source, conversion rate from organic to SQL, payback period

2. **LinkedIn Ads** (type: paid) — Account-based and cohort-based targeting at specific company titles and industries. Primary metric: Lead cost, demo requests, LTV:CAC ratio. Budget: 20–25%.
   - Focus: Retargeting engaged website visitors, lookalike audiences from current customer base, account-based campaigns on high-value target accounts
   - Measure: Cost per lead, cost per demo, demo-to-customer conversion

3. **Google Search Ads** (type: paid) — High-intent keyword bidding for product terms, competitor comparisons, and problem-aware searches. Primary metric: Lead cost, conversion rate from click to demo. Budget: 15–20%.
   - Focus: Branded keywords (protecting against competitor ads), competitor comparisons, "alternative to" keyword clusters
   - Measure: ROAS by keyword cluster, CAC by intent tier, demo booking rate

4. **Content Marketing & Customer Stories** (type: owned) — Amplified case studies, webinars, and best-practice guides. Primary metric: Organic traffic, webinar registrations, demo requests from content. Budget: 15–20%.
   - Focus: Customer case studies (with quantified metrics), webinars on trending topics, ROI calculators, competitive analysis guides
   - Measure: Lead value by content type, time to conversion from content consumption

5. **Customer Marketing & Referral Program** (type: owned) — Leverage existing customer base for referrals and brand advocacy. Primary metric: Referral-sourced revenue, NPS, customer LTV. Budget: 10–15%.
   - Focus: Structured referral program with incentives, customer ambassador program, case study rotation, user-generated content
   - Measure: Referral revenue per customer, referral CAC (should be lowest across all channels), payback period

6. **Paid Retargeting (Display/Programmatic)** (type: paid) — Re-engage warm prospects and site visitors. Primary metric: Return visitor engagement, demo requests from retargeted audience. Budget: 5–10%.
   - Focus: Website visitor list, engaged content consumers, webinar attendees
   - Measure: CAC vs. first-touch channel, multi-touch attribution value

### Transition: Organic→Paid Mix as Revenue Scales
- **At $100K ARR**: 60% organic (content + referral), 40% paid
- **At $500K ARR**: 40% organic, 50% paid, 10% customer marketing (more efficient, lower CAC)
- **At $1M+ ARR**: Shift toward customer marketing (0% CAC), Account-Based Marketing (ABM), and high-touch enterprise sales

## Unit Economics & Attribution Model

### Target Metrics
- **CAC by channel** (should vary; lowest = referrals/customer marketing; highest = early-stage brand ads)
- **LTV:CAC ratio**: ≥3:1 (minimum; 4:1+ is ideal)
- **Payback period**: <12 months (time to recoup CAC from gross margin)
- **ROAS (paid channels)**: ≥3:1 (for Google/LinkedIn ads; lower for brand awareness)
- **Magic number** (Sales Efficiency): [(MRR this month − MRR 3 months ago) × 3] / Total marketing spend (target: >0.75)

### Attribution Model
- Use **position-based (40% first touch, 40% last touch, 20% middle touches)** or **linear model** (not last-touch only)
- Reason: Last-touch overvalues bottom-funnel paid ads; misses credit for early-stage content and brand awareness
- Measure: Same customer across all touchpoints; assign credit fractionally to each channel

### Pricing & Economics Example
- **Average contract value (ACV)**: $5K–$20K/year (typical for B2B SaaS at this stage)
- **Gross margin**: 70%+ (SaaS standard)
- **CAC budget**: $500–$2,000 per customer (depends on ACV and payback tolerance)
- **LTV calculation**: (ACV × 3-year retention rate × gross margin %) / (1 + discount rate)
  - Example: ($10K ACV × 3 year × 70% margin) / 1.1 = $19K LTV
  - CAC budget: $19K ÷ 3 = $6.3K max for 3:1 ratio

## Content

### Pillars
- **How-to & best practices**: Guides and webinars solving specific workflows and challenges (e.g., "How to Build a [Process] in [Your Product Category]")
- **Competitive & comparison**: Direct comparisons vs. known competitors and incumbent solutions; ROI calculators
- **Customer success stories**: 3,000–5,000-word case studies with quantified metrics (cost savings, efficiency gains, revenue impact)
- **Thought leadership**: Founder and executive perspective on industry trends, competitive dynamics, future of category
- **Product education**: Feature deep-dives, integration guides, architecture explainers for technical audiences

### Cadence
- **Blog/SEO content**: 2–3 articles/month (mix of long-form and mid-form)
- **Case studies**: 1 new case study every 2 months (rotate through customer base)
- **Webinars**: 1–2 live webinars per month; 1–2 on-demand replays
- **LinkedIn**: 3–4 posts/week (founder, CEO, and team members—mix of insights, updates, customer wins)
- **Email nurture**: 2–3 sequences (new leads, engaged prospects, webinar attendees); send 2–3x/month
- **Guest posts**: 1–2 posts/quarter on high-authority third-party publications

### Formats
- **Long-form blog posts**: 2,000–3,500 words, SEO-optimized, with internal linking
- **Case studies**: 3,000–5,000 words + 1-page PDF with metrics; hosted on dedicated landing page
- **Webinars**: 45–60 min live sessions with Q&A; repurpose as on-demand video + blog post
- **ROI/comparison calculators**: Interactive tools (Figma, custom HTML, or calculator tool) with lead capture
- **LinkedIn posts**: 100–300 words with hook, insight, and CTA; test carousel format
- **Email templates**: Segmented by journey stage (awareness, consideration, decision)
- **Videos**: 3–8 min explainers, customer testimonials, "day in the life" content for YouTube

## Schedule

### Months 1–3: Baseline & Optimization
- **Theme**: "Establish attribution, optimize existing channels, identify quick wins."
- **Actions**:
  - Implement UTM tagging and multi-touch attribution across all channels (Google Analytics 4, Mixpanel, or HubSpot)
  - Audit existing content: identify top-performing pages by traffic and conversion rate; identify gaps (high-volume keywords you're not ranking for)
  - Launch Google Search Ads on branded keywords + top 10 competitor comparisons; budget $500–1K/month
  - Publish 3 SEO articles targeting high-intent keywords identified in Months 1–2 gap analysis
  - Build LinkedIn Ads account; launch 2 campaigns: retargeting website visitors + lookalike audience from customers; budget $300–500/month
  - Launch customer case study project: identify 3 reference customers willing to be interviewed; begin first case study write
  - Set up email nurture sequences in CRM (minimum: welcome sequence, webinar follow-up, abandoned demo sequence)
- **Success criteria**:
  - Attribution tracking live; CAC calculated for each channel
  - Top 3 content pages driving 50%+ of organic traffic identified
  - Google Ads generating 5–10 demo requests/month at <$150 CAC
  - LinkedIn Ads generating 10–15 leads/month at <$200 CAC
  - First case study published and on website
  - Email nurture conversion rate: 5%+ of new leads engaging with sequences
- **Decision point**:
  - Which channel shows lowest CAC and highest quality leads? (Indicator of where to allocate more spend)
  - Which content topics are driving the most demo requests? (Indicator of positioning and messaging that resonates)
  - Is paid channel mix (Google + LinkedIn) outperforming or underperforming organic? (Indicates need to shift budget)

### Months 4–6: Scale Winning Channels & Build Content Factory
- **Theme**: "Scale proven channels, diversify with content, establish predictable pipeline."
- **Actions**:
  - Increase Google Search Ads budget to $1K–1.5K/month (double down on best-performing keyword clusters)
  - Increase LinkedIn Ads budget to $500–750/month; test ABM campaign targeting top 50 account list
  - Launch paid content amplification: promote top-performing SEO content via LinkedIn Ads and Google Display; budget $200–300/month
  - Publish 2–3 additional SEO articles/month (6 total in this quarter); establish content calendar
  - Launch webinar series: 1 webinar every other week; themes: customer use cases, industry trends, product updates; target 30–50 registrations per webinar
  - Deploy case study #2 and #3 (stagger one every 4–5 weeks)
  - Establish customer referral program: $500–$2K incentive per referred customer; track referral source
  - Build LinkedIn thought leadership program: founder + 2 execs posting 2x/week; repurpose content from blog
- **Success criteria**:
  - Google Ads: 15–20 demo requests/month at <$100 CAC (20%+ improvement from Q1)
  - LinkedIn Ads: 25–30 leads/month at <$150 CAC
  - Organic traffic: 20% MoM growth (compound from content + SEO improvements)
  - Webinar average attendance: 25–40 attendees; 15%+ of attendees requesting demo
  - 3 customer case studies published; each generating 50+ organic views/month
  - Referral pipeline: 3–5 referral-sourced leads/month (track in CRM)
  - Email nurture: 7–10% conversion rate (improvement from Month 1–3)
  - Content backlog: 4–6 weeks of content pre-produced and scheduled
- **Decision point**:
  - Which content topic is generating the highest-quality leads? (Deep-dive into that vertical or use case)
  - Should we shift spend from LinkedIn Ads to Google Ads, or vice versa? (Based on CAC and lead quality)
  - Is webinar attendance strong enough to justify 2x/week cadence?
  - Are referrals scalable with better incentives or targeting?

### Months 7–9: Vertical Specialization & ABM Pilot
- **Theme**: "Establish credibility in a specific vertical or use case; test Account-Based Marketing."
- **Actions**:
  - Identify highest-value customer segment by ACV and churn rate; create vertical-specific content hub (6–8 articles, 2 case studies, 1 webinar)
  - Launch small ABM pilot: 20–30 target accounts identified; create targeted landing pages, email sequences, LinkedIn ads; dedicated sales outreach
  - Scale Google Search Ads budget to $2K/month; test new keyword clusters beyond branded and competitor keywords
  - Publish 2–3 vertical-specific thought leadership pieces (founder, CEO, or specialist)
  - Launch customer ambassador program: identify 5–10 customers willing to share feedback, testimonials, or speak at events; provide incentives
  - Create 2 new case studies focused on ABM pilot vertical
  - Expand LinkedIn posting to 4 team members (vs. 3); maintain 2x/week cadence per person; test LinkedIn Ads on thought leadership content
- **Success criteria**:
  - ABM pilot generating 5–10 qualified pipeline deals from target accounts
  - Vertical-specific content hub attracting 200+ organic visitors/week and driving 10%+ of total pipeline
  - Total organic pipeline: 30%+ of monthly bookings
  - Google Ads: 25–30 leads/month at <$125 CAC
  - Referral pipeline: 8–12 referral-sourced opportunities/month (measurable ROI on referral program)
  - Email nurture: 8–12% conversion rate, improving month-over-month
  - Team thought leadership generating 5K+ impressions/month on LinkedIn
- **Decision point**:
  - Is ABM vertical showing outsized CAC and quality vs. other segments? (If yes, invest more resources here)
  - Should we go all-in on vertical specialization or maintain horizontal positioning?
  - Which email nurture sequence is driving the highest conversion rate? (Expand and test variations)

### Months 10–12: Efficiency & Scaling
- **Theme**: "Optimize unit economics, prepare for scale, document playbooks."
- **Actions**:
  - Conduct full attribution audit: calculate CAC by channel, LTV:CAC ratios, payback period; identify most efficient channels for scaling
  - Scale highest-efficiency channels by 25–50% (e.g., if Google Ads has 4:1 ROAS, increase budget to $3K/month)
  - Launch 2–3 paid content amplification campaigns: repurpose top-converting landing pages, webinars, case studies; test Meta Ads for awareness-stage targeting
  - Establish content marketing operations: hire contractor or part-time resource for content execution; build 12-month content calendar
  - Scale ABM pilot: expand target account list to 50–100 accounts if Months 7–9 results justified; assign dedicated ABM manager/marketer
  - Publish annual customer report or industry benchmarking study; distribute across channels
  - Document customer acquisition playbooks: "How to Acquire a Customer in [Vertical] in 60 Days"
  - Plan Year 2 strategy: forecast revenue by channel, identify new channel experiments (events, partnerships, direct sales, etc.)
- **Success criteria**:
  - Total MRR bookings from marketing-sourced leads: 40%+ of total company bookings
  - Blended CAC: <$1,500 across all channels (varies by vertical and deal size)
  - LTV:CAC ratio: 3:1 or better
  - Payback period: <12 months
  - Organic traffic: 50% YoY growth
  - Email list: 2K+ qualified leads; nurture conversion: 8–12%
  - Referral revenue: 10–15% of total bookings
  - Paid channel ROI: 3:1+ ROAS on Google Ads + LinkedIn Ads combined
  - Team satisfaction: operations documented, playbooks written, new team member can execute playbook independently
- **Decision point**:
  - Which channels are most efficient? Which should we scale further?
  - Do we have enough data to hire a full-time marketer or agency?
  - Should we launch additional channels (e.g., events, partnerships, sales development team) in Year 2?
  - What's the gap between current metrics and target metrics? (e.g., LTV:CAC, payback period, organic %)
  - **PMF Velocity Check**: If weekly signup growth drops below 5% for 3+ consecutive weeks OR LTV:CAC falls below 2:1, pause paid scaling and investigate churn/positioning before resuming. See `framework-selection-matrix.md` §PMF Velocity Override.

## Year 2 Planning (After Months 10–12)

Once you've achieved:
- 40%+ marketing-sourced revenue
- LTV:CAC ≥3:1 payback <12 months
- Predictable, repeatable pipeline from 2–3 channels

Consider adding:
- **Events & sponsorships** (expand brand awareness, enable ABM)
- **Partnerships & reseller channels** (low-CAC distribution)
- **Sales Development team** (SDRs to work warm outbound leads)
- **Customer Success marketing** (expansion revenue from existing customers)
- **Brand/PR campaigns** (build category authority)
- **Demand Gen campaigns** (large-scale paid nurture with sequential messaging)
