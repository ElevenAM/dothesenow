# Healthtech Marketing Strategy — Bootstrap

## Goals
- Build clinical credibility and trust in a risk-averse, compliance-heavy market
- Acquire first 100 users through education, professional networks, and pilot programs
- Establish a repeatable pipeline with <$1K/mo spend focused on professional channels

## Audience
- **Primary**: Healthcare professionals (physicians, practice managers, clinic administrators) at independent practices and small groups (1–20 providers)
- **Secondary**: Health-curious consumers or patients (if B2C component exists), health system innovation teams evaluating new tools
- **Pain points**: Administrative burden, EHR frustration, regulatory compliance overhead, fragmented patient communication
- **Watering holes**: Slack groups for practice managers, LinkedIn healthcare professional communities, medical subreddits (r/medicine, r/nursing), specialty-specific forums (AAFP, MGMA)

## Channels
1. **Professional Content / SEO** (type: owned) — Evidence-based articles, workflow guides, and compliance explainers targeting healthcare-specific searches. Primary metric: Organic search traffic and pilot lead quality. Budget: 40%.
2. **Professional Networks** (type: earned) — Medical conferences (virtual), professional associations, LinkedIn groups for healthcare professionals. Primary metric: Conference referrals and association partnership agreements. Budget: 30%.
3. **Pilot Programs** (type: owned) — Free pilot with 3–5 practices in exchange for case studies and testimonials. Primary metric: Pilot conversion rate and case study outcomes. Budget: 30%.

## Compliance Gate
All marketing content requires review before publication:
- **Risk tier 1 (low)**: Workflow improvement tips, general industry news → Self-review + checklist (same-day)
- **Risk tier 2 (medium)**: Product features, efficiency claims, integration guides → Legal review (5 business days)
- **Risk tier 3 (high)**: Clinical claims, patient outcomes, case studies, pilot results → Medical advisor + legal review (5–8 business days)

⚠️ HIPAA requires written patient authorization for any case study or testimonial using PHI. De-identify per HIPAA Safe Harbor: remove names, dates, locations, ages >89. FDA scrutinizes claims using "medical grade," "clinical," or therapeutic language. Recent enforcement: $50K fine for disclosing patient PHI in a Google review response (2024).

## Content
### Pillars
- Clinical workflow improvement guides (save X hours/week)
- Compliance and regulatory explainers (HIPAA, data security, state-specific rules)
- Pilot program results and practice success stories

### Cadence
- Professional SEO articles: 1–2/month
- LinkedIn posts: 2/week
- Case studies: 1 every 8 weeks

### Formats
- Long-form evidence-based guides (1,500–2,000 words)
- Workflow improvement tip sheets (downloadable)
- Case study interviews with practice leaders (1,200–1,500 words)
- Compliance checklists and regulatory guides

## Schedule

### Months 1–2: Foundation & Pilot Recruitment
- **Actions**: Publish 3 SEO articles on workflow and compliance. Recruit 3–5 pilot practices through direct outreach and professional introductions. Start LinkedIn posting 2x/week targeting healthcare professionals.
- **Success criteria**: 100+ organic monthly visitors from SEO articles; 3+ pilot practices signed; 200+ LinkedIn followers/engagement
- **Decision point**: If pilot recruitment stalls after 4 weeks, expand to paid community ads or medical conference sponsorships

### Months 3–4: Case Studies & Association Building
- **Actions**: Produce 1–2 case studies from pilot participants (post-HIPAA review). Present at 1 virtual healthcare conference or webinar. Build email list from content leads.
- **Success criteria**: 1–2 case studies published; 50+ webinar attendees; 40+ email subscribers
- **Decision point**: If conference visibility is low, pivot to a professional association partnership instead

### Months 5–6: Directory Presence & Scaling
- **Actions**: Publish 2 additional SEO articles. Publish final pilot results formally. Apply to 2–3 healthcare directories and review sites. Explore partnership with 1 professional association for co-branded content.
- **Success criteria**: 5+ directory listings; 150+ monthly organic traffic; partnership agreement signed
- **Decision point**: If directory traffic <30 visitors/month, focus exclusive energy on SEO and professional associations
- **PMF Velocity Override**: If weekly signup growth >15% AND MRR >$500, graduate to Growth tier regardless of calendar phase. See `framework-selection-matrix.md` §PMF Velocity Override.
