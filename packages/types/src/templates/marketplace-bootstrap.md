# Marketplace Marketing Strategy — Bootstrap

## Goals
- Solve the cold-start problem by bootstrapping the supply side first
- Achieve first 100 active participants (combined supply + demand) through direct outreach and community
- Build organic demand-side acquisition with <$1K/mo spend once supply is seeded

## Audience
- **Supply side (primary first)**: Service providers, sellers, or creators who will list on the marketplace
- **Demand side**: Buyers, clients, or consumers who will transact on the marketplace
- **Pain points (supply)**: Difficulty finding customers, high platform fees elsewhere, lack of visibility
- **Pain points (demand)**: Fragmented options, trust/quality uncertainty, poor discovery
- **Watering holes (supply)**: Industry-specific Slack groups, Facebook groups for service providers, dedicated subreddits for your niche
- **Watering holes (demand)**: Problem-specific subreddits, industry forums, niche Discord communities

## Channels
1. **Direct Outreach** (type: owned) — Personal emails, LinkedIn DMs, and community posts to recruit initial supply. Primary metric: Supply-side recruitment rate and onboarding completion. Budget: 50%.
2. **SEO / Content** (type: owned) — Long-tail content targeting problem-aware searches from buyers. Primary metric: Organic search traffic to demand-side content. Budget: 30%.
3. **Community** (type: earned) — Niche forums, Slack/Discord groups, subreddits where supply and demand already congregate. Primary metric: Referral traffic and participation rate. Budget: 20%.

## Content
### Pillars
- Supply-side success stories ("How [provider] grew their business on [platform]")
- Buyer guides and curated "best of" lists (SEO magnets for demand)
- Marketplace transparency content (pricing, quality standards, how vetting works)

### Cadence
- Demand-side SEO articles: 1/month
- Supply-side success stories: 1 every 6 weeks
- Transparency/update posts: 2/month

### Formats
- Long-form SEO buyer guides (1,500+ words)
- Provider success story case studies (800–1,200 words)
- Short-form transparency posts (LinkedIn, email updates)
- Listing optimization tips (guides for supply side)

## Schedule

### Months 1–3: Supply-Side Recruitment (Cold Start)
- **Actions**: Personally recruit 30–50 supply-side participants through targeted direct outreach. Create detailed onboarding guide and listing optimization playbook. Establish 1–2 community partnerships.
- **Success criteria**: 30+ active supply-side participants; onboarding guide published; 2+ community referral partners identified
- **Decision point**: If recruitment pace <10 new providers/month, expand to paid community ads or influencer outreach on platforms where supply congregates

### Months 4–6: Demand Acquisition & Retention
- **Actions**: Launch 2–3 demand-side SEO articles. Build email list from content leads. Publish 1 supply-side success story. Launch supply-side referral program (existing providers recruit new ones).
- **Success criteria**: 200+ monthly organic visitors; 50+ email subscribers; 1 published case study; referral program driving 20% of new supply
- **Decision point**: If SEO content <100 monthly visitors after 6 weeks, reallocate to community strategy or partnership activation instead
- **PMF Velocity Override**: If weekly transaction growth >15% AND GMV >$500/month, graduate to Growth tier regardless of calendar phase. See `framework-selection-matrix.md` §PMF Velocity Override.
