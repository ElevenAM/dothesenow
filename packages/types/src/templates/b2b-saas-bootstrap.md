# B2B SaaS Marketing Strategy — Bootstrap

## Goals
- Establish product-market fit signal through organic acquisition
- Build a repeatable inbound pipeline with <$1K/mo spend
- Achieve first 100 users through content and community

## Audience
- **Primary**: Technical decision-makers (CTOs, VP Eng, senior devs) at startups (10–100 employees)
- **Secondary**: Non-technical founders evaluating tools for their teams
- **Pain points**: Manual processes, tool sprawl, lack of visibility into team output
- **Watering holes**: Hacker News, Twitter/X (#buildinpublic), LinkedIn, GitHub discussions, dev-focused Slack communities

## Channels
1. **Content/SEO** (type: owned) — Long-tail blog posts targeting problem-aware searches. Primary metric: organic signups. Budget: 35%.
2. **LinkedIn** (type: owned) — Founder-led thought leadership and cold outreach to decision-makers. Primary metric: profile visits → inbound. Budget: 25%.
3. **Product-Led Growth** (type: owned) — Free tier with viral loops (invite teammates, share reports). Primary metric: viral coefficient / team signup rate. Budget: 20%.
4. **Cold Email Outreach** (type: earned/paid) — Founder-led outreach to target accounts with personalized use-case demos. Primary metric: reply rate, meeting booked rate. Budget: 20%.

## Content

### Pillars
- How-to guides solving specific workflow pain points
- Comparison posts (vs. manual process, vs. incumbent tools)
- Customer stories / case studies (even early design partners)
- Founder brand: founder's perspective on engineering ops, team visibility, tool selection

### Cadence
- Blog/SEO: 4 high-quality articles per 2 months, each repurposed into 2-3 LinkedIn posts
- LinkedIn: 2-3 founder posts per week (mix of tips, opinions, customer spotlights)
- Email: 2x/month to blog subscribers (case studies, new articles, product updates)

### Formats
- Long-form blog posts (1500–2500 words, SEO-optimized)
- LinkedIn short-form (150–300 words) with founder voice
- Case study mini-sites (5 company profile, problem, solution, metrics)
- Email newsletters (curated tips + new content)

## Schedule

### Months 1–2: Foundation & Founder Launch
- **Actions**: Publish 4 SEO articles on top pain points. Set up LinkedIn posting cadence (2-3x/week). Launch free tier with team invite loop. Begin cold email list building and outreach templates.
- **Success criteria**: 500 blog visitors/month, 100+ email subscribers, 20+ free signups, 2-3 cold email replies.
- **Decision point**: Which content topic is driving the most signups? Is founder-led cold outreach resonating? Adjust focus for Months 3-4.

### Months 3–4: Scale Validation
- **Actions**: Publish 4 more SEO articles on top-performing topics. Run customer interview series for case studies. Scale cold email volume 2x. Start email nurture to free tier users.
- **Success criteria**: 1,500+ blog visitors/month, 300+ email subscribers, 50+ free signups, 5-7 cold email replies/week, 2 case study candidates identified.
- **Decision point**: Which channel is delivering highest-quality signups? Is there sufficient product-market fit signal to justify paid ads? Plan Months 5-6 focus.

### Months 5–6: Growth & Optimization
- **Actions**: Launch referral program with extended trial incentive. Guest post on 2-3 industry blogs. Publish final batch of 4 articles. Optimize top-performing cold email sequences.
- **Success criteria**: 2,500+ blog visitors/month, 500+ email subscribers, 100+ free signups, 5-8 paid conversions, referral signups tracking.
- **Decision point**: Do we have sufficient PMF to graduate from bootstrap to paid acquisition? Which channels to invest in next?
- **PMF Velocity Override**: If weekly signup growth >15% AND MRR >$500, graduate to Growth tier regardless of calendar phase. See `framework-selection-matrix.md` §PMF Velocity Override.
