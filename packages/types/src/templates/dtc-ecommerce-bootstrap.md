# DTC eCommerce Marketing Strategy — Bootstrap

## Goals
- Build a direct relationship with customers through owned channels (email, SMS)
- Drive first 100 orders through targeted social and content marketing
- Establish a repeatable acquisition loop with <$1K/mo spend

## Audience
- **Primary**: End consumers aged 25–45 who shop online and follow brands on social media
- **Secondary**: Gift buyers and trend-conscious shoppers discovering new brands via Instagram/TikTok
- **Pain points**: Decision fatigue, lack of trust in new brands, desire for authentic brand stories
- **Watering holes**: Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, Facebook Groups (niche communities), email newsletters (shopping, lifestyle)

## Channels
1. **Email/SMS** (type: owned) — Welcome series, abandoned cart, post-purchase flows, VIP list. Primary metric: email revenue %, SMS opt-in rate. Budget: 30%.
2. **Meta Ads** (type: paid) — Low-budget retargeting and lookalike campaigns on Instagram/Facebook. Primary metric: ROAS, cost per acquisition. Budget: 35%.
3. **Organic Social** (type: owned) — Instagram Reels, TikTok content, UGC reposts, founder/brand storytelling. Primary metric: engagement rate, saves, shares. Budget: 20%.
4. **UGC & Influencer Seeding** (type: paid/earned) — Micro-influencer product seeding, UGC creator partnerships, brand hashtag campaigns. Primary metric: UGC submissions, influencer reach, conversion. Budget: 15%.

## Content

### Pillars
- Behind-the-scenes / founder story content
- Product education and use-case demonstrations
- Customer testimonials and unboxing UGC
- Lifestyle and aspirational content (how customers use products in their lives)

### Cadence
- Instagram: 3-4 posts per week (mix of product, lifestyle, UGC, founder story)
- TikTok: 2-3 videos per week (trending audio, product demos, challenges, UGC)
- Email: 2-3x per week (welcome series, promotional, product education)
- SMS: 1x per week (flash offers, restocks, VIP exclusives)

### Formats
- Instagram Reels and static posts (product photos, lifestyle, founder snapshots)
- TikTok short-form video (15–60 seconds, trending sounds, behind-the-scenes)
- Email campaigns (welcome series, abandoned cart, review requests, cross-sell, newsletter)
- SMS messages (limited time offers, restock alerts, VIP access)
- UGC (customer photos, testimonials, unboxing videos, hashtag submissions)

## Schedule

### Months 1–2: Foundation & Email Infrastructure
- **Actions**: Set up email capture (popup + footer form, target 5% conversion). Build 3-email welcome series and abandoned cart recovery flow. Post 3x/week on Instagram (product + lifestyle). Create branded hashtag and encourage UGC. Begin SMS list building (opt-in gate with 10% discount offer).
- **Success criteria**: 500+ email subscribers, 200+ SMS opt-ins, 10+ first orders, 2-3% email welcome series conversion, 1,000+ Instagram followers, 5-10 UGC submissions.
- **Decision point**: Are email sequences converting? What Instagram content format drives the most engagement? Is UGC creation realistic at current scale?

### Months 3–4: Paid Amplification & Scale
- **Actions**: Launch first Meta retargeting campaign ($5–10/day budget). Increase Instagram posting to 4x/week. Create 2-3 TikTok videos to test organic reach. Implement SMS promotional flow (weekly VIP offer or restock alert). Reach out to 3-5 micro-influencers for product seeding partnerships.
- **Success criteria**: $200+ daily revenue from Meta ads, 3-5% ROAS, 50+ orders/month, 100+ TikTok followers, 2-3 influencer partnerships active, 1,500+ email subscribers, SMS revenue tracking.
- **Decision point**: Is Meta ROAS sustainable? Which organic social format (Instagram Reels vs. TikTok) drives better engagement? Do influencer partnerships deliver acceptable payback?

### Months 5–6: Lifecycle & Optimization
- **Actions**: Build post-purchase email flow (review request → cross-sell → loyalty). Optimize best-performing TikTok and Instagram content. Scale successful Meta campaigns by 20-30%. Establish monthly UGC collection process. Test SMS-only offers for VIP segments.
- **Success criteria**: 5-8% ROAS on Meta ads, 100+ orders/month, 20%+ repeat purchase rate, 3,000+ email subscribers, 2,000+ SMS subscribers, 10%+ cross-sell rate, 20-30 UGC submissions/month.
- **Decision point**: Is the business sustainably profitable at current unit economics? Which channels/tactics should receive increased investment in next phase?
- **PMF Velocity Override**: If weekly order growth >15% AND monthly revenue >$500, graduate to Growth tier regardless of calendar phase. See `framework-selection-matrix.md` §PMF Velocity Override.

## Unit Economics Target (DTC)

- **Repeat Purchase Rate**: 25%+ (target)
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) : Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: 3:1+ (minimum viable for sustainability)
