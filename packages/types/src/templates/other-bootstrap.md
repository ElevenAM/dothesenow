# Other Industry Marketing Strategy — Bootstrap

⚠️ **CUSTOMIZATION REQUIRED**: This is a generic fallback template. Every section below is a starting point for discovery—not a prescription. Your actual marketing strategy will depend on your specific business model, target audience, and competitive landscape. Work through the Discovery phase (Months 1–2) to validate assumptions before scaling spend.

## Goals
- Validate core positioning and messaging through customer conversations
- Identify the highest-leverage acquisition channel(s) for your business
- Acquire first 10–20 customers through repeatable, founder-led GTM motion
- Build foundational customer data to inform paid acquisition in future phases

## Channels

### Discovery Phase (Months 1–2): Manual & Earned Only
1. **Direct Outreach** (type: earned) — Founder-led cold email, calls, or in-person conversations with 30–50 target customers. Primary metric: Response rate, learning quality (what positioning resonates?), conversion to customers. Budget: 50%.
2. **Content Experiments** (type: owned) — Write 2–4 exploratory blog posts or guides addressing your audience's top pain points. Primary metric: Traffic, time-on-page, inbound inquiries. Budget: 25%.
3. **Community Engagement** (type: earned) — Active participation in relevant Slack communities, Reddit, forums, or local meetups. Primary metric: Conversation quality, referrals generated. Budget: 25%.

## Content

### Pillars
- **Customer discovery**: Document what you learn in direct conversations—what problems do they face? Translate into content.
- **Positioning testing**: Write about different angles on your value prop and measure which resonates.
- **Educational/problem-focused**: Guides or explainers addressing your audience's top pain points (independent of your product).
- **Build-in-public**: Document your journey and learnings (builds credibility with early adopters).

### Cadence
- **Direct outreach**: 10–15 contacts/week (Months 1–2); increase to 20–30/week if validating well (Months 3–4)
- **Blog/content**: 1–2 posts/month exploring different positioning angles
- **Community**: 2–3 hours/week active engagement in target communities
- **Email (when list exists)**: 2x/month sharing learnings, early wins, or content

### Formats
- Personal emails with clear value prop and ask (not templates yet—customize each one)
- Blog posts (800–1,500 words, problem-focused, not salesy)
- Discussion posts in communities (authentic questions and perspective-sharing)
- LinkedIn short-form or Twitter thread (founder perspective on industry challenges)
- Case studies from early customers (even design partners; even 200 words is valuable)

## Schedule

### Months 1–2: Discovery & Customer Development
- **Theme**: "What problem are we solving? Who has it? How do they talk about it?"
- **Actions**:
  - Make 40–50 direct outreach attempts to target customers (cold email, LinkedIn DM, warm intro, phone)
  - Document learnings from every conversation: positioning language that resonates, objections, alternate use cases
  - Publish 2 exploratory blog posts testing different angles on your value prop
  - Join 2–3 communities where your audience hangs out; ask questions, respond to problems
  - Create simple one-pager or Google Doc summarizing: "What we learned about customer pain and positioning"
- **Success criteria**:
  - 10%+ response rate on direct outreach (or 5+ meaningful conversations)
  - Identify 1–2 clear customer pain points that multiple people mention unprompted
  - 2–3 customers willing to be design partners or give testimonials
  - 200+ organic visitors to exploratory blog posts
  - Clear winner emerging: which positioning angle generated the most interest?
- **Decision point**:
  - Do we have enough signal that our positioning is valuable? (E.g., multiple people volunteering to pay or commit time)
  - Which channel generated the best-quality leads in Month 1–2? (e.g., cold email vs. content vs. communities)
  - Is there a repeatable manual sales motion? (E.g., "Direct outreach → 2 follow-ups → conversion to paid pilot")

### Months 3–4: Scale Validation & Channel Testing
- **Theme**: "Can we repeatably acquire customers? Which channel scales best?"
- **Actions**:
  - Scale direct outreach to 20–30 contacts/week (based on Months 1–2 learnings—reuse successful templates, messages, positioning)
  - Launch paid experiment on highest-confidence channel from Month 1–2 (e.g., $200/month Google Ads, LinkedIn, or social)
  - Publish 2 more blog posts doubling down on winning positioning angles from Months 1–2
  - Begin email nurture sequence to prospects who didn't convert: share new content, case studies, social proof
  - Document unit economics: Cost to acquire one customer, time to close, contract value
- **Success criteria**:
  - 5–10 customers acquired from direct outreach (at repeatable cost and timeline)
  - Paid channel experiment generates 10%+ of manual channel cost-per-acquisition (or better)
  - 500+ organic blog visitors/month
  - Email nurture sequence converts 5%+ of contacted prospects
  - Clear quantified GTM motion: "X outreach attempts → Y meetings → Z customers at $[cost]"
- **Decision point**:
  - Is there enough signal to justify paid acquisition? (i.e., organic/manual channels working well, repeatable, better than paid startup cost)
  - Should we double down on the winning channel from Months 1–2, or pivot?
  - Is there enough product-market fit to move beyond founder-led sales?

### Months 5–6: Optimization & Future Planning
- **Theme**: "Optimize our best channel, build a case for scaling."
- **Actions**:
  - Scale winning channel (direct outreach, content, community, or paid) based on Months 3–4 learnings
  - Launch 2–3 customer referral incentives or case study campaigns (leverage early wins for credibility)
  - If paid channel performing well: incrementally increase budget to 2–3x Month 3–4 spend
  - Document everything: Customer acquisition cost (CAC), customer lifetime value (LTV), payback period, churn
  - Prepare positioning and messaging playbook for future team/agency hires
- **Success criteria**:
  - 15–25 total customers acquired (clear path to 100+ in next 6 months)
  - Unit economics validated: CAC <25% of first-year revenue, payback <6 months
  - Winning channel showing 2x improvement in cost or conversion vs. Month 3–4
  - 3–5 case studies or customer testimonials documented
- **Decision point**:
  - Do we have enough traction and repeatability to graduate from bootstrap to scaled acquisition? (e.g., hire marketer, run paid ads)
  - Which 1–2 channels should we focus on scaling in next phase?
  - Should we optimize for speed (volume) or efficiency (LTV:CAC) in next phase?

## Next Steps (After Bootstrap Phase)

Once you've validated your positioning, proven a repeatable GTM motion, and acquired 20+ customers, you're ready to:
1. **Hire or outsource marketing operations** (dedicated person or agency to handle outreach, content, campaigns)
2. **Scale your winning channel(s)** with increased budget ($1K–$5K/month)
3. **Diversify channels** (if primary channel is manual, add content/SEO or paid ads)
4. **Implement attribution** (track which channel each customer came from; measure CAC by source)
5. **Refine unit economics** (measure LTV:CAC ratio, payback period, retention)

Until then: **stay founder-led, stay lean, stay curious.**
